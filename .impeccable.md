## Design Context

### Users
Players patching Pokémon Elite Redux quickly on desktop or mobile. They come here with a clean Emerald ROM and want the correct patch with as little friction and uncertainty as possible.

### Brand Personality
Confident, clean, gamey. The interface should feel trustworthy and fast first, with just enough Pokémon-hack personality to feel specific rather than generic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark theme is the right default for this audience and use case. Keep the existing Elite Redux teal accent as the anchor, but use it with restraint against darker, slightly tinted neutrals. The page should feel like a polished game utility, not a dashboard, not a crypto tool, and not a generic glowing gamer UI.

### Design Principles
1. Make the main action obvious immediately: patch the ROM with the right release.
2. Keep current releases primary and archive releases secondary, but still easy to reach.
3. Prefer clarity over decoration; every visual detail should help confidence, scanning, or hierarchy.
4. Preserve a game-adjacent feel without adding noisy effects, clutter, or gimmicks.
5. Make the page feel solid on both desktop and mobile, with no hidden critical actions.
