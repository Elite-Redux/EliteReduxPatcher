What is Pokémon Elite Redux?

Pokémon Elite Redux is a challenging ROM hack based on Pokémon Emerald, designed for players who love strategic battling and team building without the grind. Its most unique feature allows Pokémon to have up to four abilities active simultaneously (switchable and innate), opening up countless new strategies and replayability.

The game features:

  • Multi-Ability System: Up to 3 switchable and 3 fixed (innate) abilities per Pokémon.
  • Enhanced Difficulty: Every trainer has custom competitive movesets and smarter AI. Multiple difficulty modes are available.
  • Massive Quality of Life (QoL): Zero grinding (easy EV/nature/ability changes, level caps, unlimited leveling), auto-healing, improved UI, infinite-use Poké Balls with 100% catch rate, and much more.
  • Expanded Content: Includes Pokémon from Gens 1-9+, Mega Evolutions, new Redux forms, over 500 new custom abilities, and rebalanced moves/items.
  • Customization: Choose starters from Gens 1-9 and all types, easily manage your team and items.

Essentially, Elite Redux offers a completely overhauled Emerald experience focused on challenging battles, strategic depth, and player convenience.
